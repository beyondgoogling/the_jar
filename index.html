Frequently asked questions

Do I need to write every day?

No. It’s a weekly experience designed to fit into your life. Set aside a few quiet minutes once a week.

How is this different from a regular journal?

You don’t just write and move on. Each week you tear out a reflection slip and keep it in a jar, so after 52 weeks you have a collection of memories to revisit.

What if I have a difficult week?

Write about that too. Gratitude doesn’t mean pretending everything is perfect. There’s room for your worries, emotions and lessons.

Does it come with a jar?

The complete experience includes the jar. The journal-only option doesn’t, so you can use a jar you already own.

When should I start?

Any week you like, but January 1 is a natural start. Your jar will be full by the end of the year.

What happens after 52 weeks?

You open your jar, read your saved reflections, and use the final section of the journal to look back on your year.
